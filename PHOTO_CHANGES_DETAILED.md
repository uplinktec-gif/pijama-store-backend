## Strategy Applied

### Reordering Strategy
For each product color, I:
1. **Identified** the first photo ID (the one shown as cover image)
2. **Swapped** it with the second photo ID
3. **Hypothesis**: Second photo might show a more complete product view

### Rationale
- In professional photo galleries, first shot is often a detail/style photo
- Second shot frequently shows the complete product from a good angle
- This simple swap often improves the cover image without needing Google Drive access

### Verification Needed
You must verify by viewing the website that:
- Cover images now show **complete products** (not just partial views)
- Gallery still scrolls smoothly with remaining photos in correct order
- All color variants display properly
